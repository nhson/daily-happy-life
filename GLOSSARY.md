# daily-happy-life

A purpose-aware task manager: every task traces back to a life direction drawn from *Designing Your Life* and *How to Live a Meaningful Life*.

## Life direction

**Compass**:
The user's pair of short statements — Workview and Lifeview — describing what work is for and what life is for. Rewritten yearly, as the first step of the Quarterly Review that opens the year; each pair it replaces is kept as a past Compass.
_Avoid_: Mission, vision, values

**Workview**:
The user's statement of what work is for and what good work means to them.

**Lifeview**:
The user's statement of what life is for and how the pieces of it fit together.

**Odyssey Plan**:
One of exactly three alternative five-year lives the user designs, each with a short title (six words; in Vietnamese 6–10 syllables), the questions it tests, and four gauge ratings.
_Avoid_: Scenario, path, goal

**Current Path**:
Odyssey Plan slot 1 — the life the user is already living, extended five years. Exists implicitly before the user writes it out.

**Active Plan**:
The one Odyssey Plan the user is currently pursuing; objectives hang under it. Switching it settles both sides at once: each still-active Objective of the old plan is Dropped or kept under the new plan, and the new plan's open Prototypes become Objective tasks (under a new Objective, or an existing one when three are already active) or are Closed. The switch is itself offered as a Decision to record.
_Avoid_: Main plan, primary plan

**Gauge**:
A 0–10 self-rating of an Odyssey Plan on one of four dimensions: Resources, Likability, Confidence, Coherence.
_Not to be confused with_: the Coherence Source of Meaning — the Gauge rates whether a plan fits the user's Compass.

**Compass Line**:
The single sentence shown on a given day, drawn in rotation from the Workview, Lifeview, Active Plan title, or a Mindset.
_Avoid_: Quote, affirmation, daily message

**Mindset**:
One of the five "Designer's Way" attitudes from *How to Live a Meaningful Life*: Wonder, Availability, Radical Acceptance, Fully Engaged & Calmly Detached, Create Your World.
_Not to be confused with_: the Wonder Source of Meaning — the Mindset is an attitude to practise; the Source is something a day touched.

## Work

**Objective**:
A result the user commits to for one quarter under the Active Plan; at most three active at a time.
_Avoid_: Goal, OKR, project

**Task**:
A single doable action, always of exactly one Task Kind.
_Avoid_: Todo, item, action

**Done** / **Closed**:
The two ways a Task ends. Done means it was completed and counts toward the Life Share; Closed means it ended without being done — the user let it go, its Objective was Dropped or finished, or its Prototype was abandoned — and stays only as history. Removing a Task created by mistake is neither: it leaves no trace.
_Avoid_: Deleted, archived, cancelled

**Task Kind**:
The "why" of a task: Objective (serves an Objective), Maintenance (keeps life running), or Prototype (tests a non-active Odyssey Plan).

**Maintenance**:
A task that serves no life direction but must be done (taxes, groceries).
_Avoid_: Chore, admin, misc

**Prototype**:
A cheap real-world experiment that tests a non-active Odyssey Plan — a conversation, a trial weekend.
_Avoid_: Experiment, test, trial

**Top 3**:
The up-to-three tasks the user chooses as most important for a given Logical Day. Chosen fresh each morning; unfinished ones from the most recent Logical Day that had a Top 3 (looking back at most a week) are offered, not carried automatically. Each day's Top 3 is remembered as it stood when the day ended.
_Avoid_: Priorities, MITs, focus list

**Life Share**:
The split of completed tasks over the last seven Logical Days across Objective, Maintenance and Prototype.
_Avoid_: Ratio, score, alignment

**Quarter Close**:
The moment at the start of a new quarter when each still-active Objective is marked Done, Dropped, or Carried Forward. A Carried Forward Objective takes its open Tasks into the new quarter; a Dropped Objective's open Tasks are Closed. Until it is done, last quarter's still-active Objectives stay in use. It ends by asking whether the user made any Decision that quarter.
_Avoid_: Rollover, reset

**Quarterly Review**:
At the start of a quarter, one look back at the quarter just ended: the Life Dashboard, the Odyssey Plans' Gauges re-rated, the Active Plan kept or switched, then Quarter Close of its leftovers and the Decision question. It contains Quarter Close.
_Avoid_: Quarterly report, OKR review

**Life Dashboard**:
Four 0–10 self-ratings of how full each part of life is: Health, Work, Play, Love. Rated once a quarter, in the Quarterly Review.
_Not to be confused with_: the Gauges, which rate an Odyssey Plan, not the life being lived.

## Daily ritual

**Logical Day**:
The user's day, which ends at their chosen "day ends at" hour (default 04:00) rather than at midnight; a check-in at 00:40 belongs to the previous Logical Day.
_Avoid_: Date, calendar day

**Morning Ritual**:
The ~2-minute start of day: read the Compass Line, pick the Top 3, write an Intention.

**Intention**:
A single if-then plan for the day ("When I ___, I will ___").
_Avoid_: Goal, resolution, plan

**Evening Check-in**:
The ~60-second end of day: tick done tasks, rate Energy and Engagement, tap Sources of Meaning, optional note. If missed, it can be completed once, the next morning, for the previous Logical Day only — ratings, Sources of Meaning and note, but not ticking tasks.
_Avoid_: Journal, review, reflection

**Energy** / **Engagement**:
1–5 self-ratings from the Good Time Journal: how energised the day left you, and how absorbed you were.

**Source of Meaning**:
One of Wonder, Coherence, Flow, Community — what a day touched that made it feel meaningful.
_Not to be confused with_: the Wonder Mindset or the Coherence Gauge.

**Focus Session**:
A stretch of undivided time the user gives one Task, of a length chosen up front. When it ends, the user says whether the Task is Done, goes another round, or leaves it for now.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, sprint, timer

**Daily Log**:
The single record of one day's Intention and Evening Check-in.

**Weekly Review**:
Once a week, after the week ends on Sunday: the week's numbers, a few short notes on its patterns, one question to sit with, and one line the user keeps.
_Avoid_: Weekly report, retrospective, summary

**Decision**:
A real life choice the user made, recorded with what drove it and optionally the Odyssey Plan it relates to.
_Avoid_: Choice, journal entry
